# 2026.243.1139 — feat(punktfunk): add stream-probe, the assertion that video actually decodes

## Summary

Adds `stream-probe`, the `punktfunk:` verb method that asserts video actually **decodes**.

Everything shipped before this could have passed while punktfunk could not stream at all. The
fleet bed proved installation, pairing and the control plane, but nothing counted a decoded
frame — so a host that paired and then produced no video would still have gone green.
`stream-probe` holds a session open for `stream_for`, counts decoded VIDEO frames, and passes
or fails on that count rather than on the CLI's exit code, because the client exits non-zero on
a clean teardown and an exit-code check would have been decorative.
